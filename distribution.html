Service

Distribution built on routing, not spray-and-pray.

Two releases with identical copy can have wildly different outcomes because of where they were sent. Tiering, vertical matching and geography decide whether a journalist sees your story or scrolls past it.

Targeting

Filters that change the outcome

Industry vertical

One primary vertical and up to three secondary routes. A fintech release can ride financial desks and technology desks without being dumped into a general news bucket.

Geography

Country, region, state or metro. Regional targeting typically lifts local pickup rates by 30–60% compared with untargeted distribution.

Language

24 languages including Arabic, Hebrew and Hindi, each with an independent indexable page and native-speaker review.

Multimedia

Images, video embeds, audio and PDF media kits. Rich media increases time-on-page and the odds of a desk picking the story up.

Embargo and scheduling

Hold until a precise minute and timezone, or schedule a coordinated multi-release rollout across regions.

Presets and repeat routing

Save a routing recipe per brand or client and reuse it. Agency teams cut submission time roughly in half.

Delivery guarantees

What we commit to in writing

Contracted lane delivery

If your release is approved and paid, it is delivered to every lane in your selected tier. No "up to" language.

A published pickup log

Every destination that received the release is listed in your dashboard, with a timestamp.

No fake outlets

We do not pad a network with auto-generated domains that exist only to host syndicated copy.

Monitoring from minute one

Pickup detection starts at publication, not the next morning, and updates live.

Backlink transparency

Every earned link is reported with follow status. We do not count nofollow syndication as link equity.

A human to call

Named account contact on Amplify and above, during and after the campaign.
