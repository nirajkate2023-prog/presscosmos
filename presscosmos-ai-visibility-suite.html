PressCosmos launches AI Visibility Suite with share-of-answer tracking across nine engines

PressCosmos released an AI Visibility Suite that formats every press release for answer engines and reports how often a brand is cited across nine AI platforms.

All releasesPublish your own

SAN FRANCISCO, CA, UNITED STATES — October 15, 2026

PressCosmos today launched its AI Visibility Suite, a set of formatting and measurement features applied to every press release distributed through the platform. The suite includes answer-engine formatted summary blocks, Organization and NewsArticle structured data, and share-of-answer tracking across nine AI systems.

Share-of-answer reporting measures how often, and how accurately, a brand appears in responses to a tracked question set on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Google AI Overviews, Meta AI, Grok and You.com.

The company also published an open crawler policy and an llms.txt guidance file, and said the formatting features are included on all plans, including the $149 entry tier.

“Distribution solved getting published. It never solved getting repeated. If an assistant answers a category question without naming you, the release did not finish its job.”

— Dr. Lian Chen, VP, Search and AI Products, PressCosmos

About PressCosmos Media Network

PressCosmos is a press release distribution and AI visibility platform serving organisations in 68 countries. Founded in 2026 and headquartered in San Francisco, the company distributes releases on a pay-per-release basis from $149.
