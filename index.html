The difference

Getting published is step one. Being repeated is the point.

A press release that nobody reads is a PDF with a press release at the top. PressCosmos optimises the four things that decide whether your announcement travels:

  • Retrievability — clean HTML, canonical URLs, a sitemap and an open crawler policy so machines can read you.
  • Answerability — a direct answer to the reader's obvious question in the first 60 words, so an assistant has something quotable.
  • Attribution — your organisation named consistently as an entity, with a structured knowledge signal behind it.
  • Corroboration — syndication across independent outlets, which is still the strongest signal that a claim is worth repeating.
