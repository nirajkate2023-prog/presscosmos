Pricing

Published prices. No quote wall.

Every plan includes human editorial review, a permanent hosted release page and pickup reporting. Pay per release, or upgrade to Studio if you are publishing every week.

Submit a releaseCompare with other wires
Quick answer PressCosmos costs $149 per release on the Launch plan, $349 on Amplify, $899 on Newsmaker, or $1,990 per month on Studio for up to twelve releases. There are no per-seat fees and no annual contract required for per-release plans. All plans include editorial review and permanent hosting.

Volume

Agency and enterprise pricing

If you distribute ten or more releases a month, list pricing stops being sensible. Volume pricing starts 22% below list and scales with commitment.

Agency workspaces add multi-client separation, white-label reporting and shared routing presets. Enterprise adds SAML SSO, approval chains, audit logging, custom retention and a signed data processing agreement.

  • Volume discount from 10 releases per month
  • White-label dashboards for agency clients
  • SSO, SCIM and audit logging on enterprise
  • DPA, custom retention and regional data residency
  • Invoice and PO billing on annual terms
  • Named onboarding and quarterly business review

PressCosmos is a press release distribution and AI visibility platform. You write or upload an announcement, our editors review it, and we syndicate it to newswire partners, trade media, regional press and AI answer engines while tracking every pickup, backlink and AI citation you earn.

Traditional wires optimise for a press release existing on the internet. PressCosmos optimises for your announcement being found, quoted and repeated — by journalists, by search engines and by AI assistants. Every release ships with answer-engine formatting, structured data and entity markup by default.
