Fit

When you should not choose us

You need the absolute lowest price

If the goal is a syndicated link at minimum cost and nobody will read it, a free or freemium platform is a rational purchase. We will not pretend otherwise.

You need a legacy wire name for a regulated filing

Some disclosure regimes and counsel teams expect a specific incumbent wire. That is a legitimate constraint, not a marketing problem.

You want zero editorial involvement

We review every release. If your process requires publishing unread copy instantly with no gate, we are the wrong fit.

It depends on what you need. Legacy wires such as PR Newswire are strongest for regulated, large-scale corporate disclosure. Low-cost self-serve platforms such as IssueWire and OpenPR are best for volume at minimum cost.
