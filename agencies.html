Workflow

What changes when you move agency distribution here

Client workspaces

Isolated media lists, presets, users and reporting per client. No accidental cross-client asset sharing, and no separate logins to administer.

Routing presets

Save a client's tier, verticals, metros and approval chain once, then reuse it. Agency teams typically halve submission time.

White-label reporting

Your logo, colours and domain on shared report links. Includes the AI citation section clients ask about and competitors cannot show.

Approval chains

Route through your client's legal or compliance reviewer without leaving the platform, with read receipts for the record.

API and bulk submission

Programmatic submission for high-volume accounts plus CSV bulk import, so a 40-client month does not become forty manual uploads.

Economics

What agencies usually save

Margin on pass-through

Distribution is billed per client at your rate. Volume pricing below list is where agency margin comes from.

Time recovered

Presets, bulk import and saved approval chains remove the repetitive work from every submission.

Retention evidence

White-label reports with citation tracking give clients a reason to renew that a pickup list alone does not.
