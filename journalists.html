For journalists

Story material, without the paywall or the signup.

Every release we distribute is public and free to use with attribution. If you need data, an interview or a document, ask the desk directly.

Browse the feedRSS
Quick answer PressCosmos provides journalists with a free, searchable press release feed, an RSS subscription, filterable archives by industry and date, media asset access, and a direct editorial desk at newsroom@presscosmos.com for data requests, interviews and verification.

Tips

Getting the most out of our feed

Filter, do not scroll

Industry and date filters plus keyword search cover the full archive. The feed runs to thousands of releases a year.

Subscribe to a slice

The RSS feed is the whole firehose. Contact us if you want a filtered feed for a specific beat or region.

Ask for the primary source

For any figure in a release, the desk can usually provide the underlying source document.
