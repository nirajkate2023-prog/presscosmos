General

PressCosmos is a press release distribution and AI visibility platform. You write or upload an announcement, our editors review it, and we syndicate it to newswire partners, trade media, regional press and AI answer engines while tracking every pickup, backlink and AI citation you earn.

Traditional wires optimise for a press release existing on the internet. PressCosmos optimises for your announcement being found, quoted and repeated — by journalists, by search engines and by AI assistants. Every release ships with answer-engine formatting, structured data and entity markup by default.

Median time from approved submission to first live pickup is 42 minutes on Amplify and Newsmaker plans. Launch plan releases go live within four business hours, and the crisis lane publishes in under 60 minutes at any hour.

Yes. Every release passes a human editorial review for accuracy, newsworthiness, disclosure requirements and formatting. We approve 98% of submissions, usually with suggested improvements rather than rejections.

PressCosmos supports 24 languages including Arabic, Hebrew and Hindi in right-to-left and complex scripts. Translation is handled by specialist PR translators, not generic engines, and each language variant gets its own indexable page.

We offer a free review and a limited free tier for qualifying non-profits, student projects and public-interest announcements. Paid plans start at $149 per release with no subscription required.

Every plan includes a hosted analytics dashboard with a live pickup map, syndicated URL list, backlink inventory, referral traffic, sentiment scoring and — on Amplify and above — AI share-of-answer tracking across nine answer engines.

We guarantee delivery to our contracted network lanes and we publish a live pickup log for every release. We do not guarantee that an individual journalist will write about you, because no ethical distributor can honestly promise editorial coverage.

AI visibility

AEO is Answer Engine Optimisation — structuring content so it can be lifted as a direct answer. GEO is Generative Engine Optimisation — being cited inside AI-generated responses. LLMO is Large Language Model Optimisation — making your brand factually retrievable and correctly described by models. PressCosmos applies all three to every release.

We publish each release as clean semantic HTML with NewsArticle and Organization structured data, a plain-language summary block, stable canonical URLs and machine-readable feeds, then allow the major AI crawlers in robots.txt. Combined, those signals make your announcement retrievable, quotable and attributable.

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Google AI Overviews, Meta AI, Grok and You.com. Share-of-answer tracking reports how often and how accurately your brand appears in responses for your tracked question set.

Distribution
