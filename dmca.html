Legal

Copyright Policy and DMCA

If your work appears on a PressCosmos page without permission, here is exactly how to get it removed.

Template notice This page is a working template written for the PressCosmos launch build. It is drafted to be accurate and readable, but it is not legal advice. Have your counsel review it against the jurisdictions you operate in before you rely on it commercially. Last reviewed: 2026-10-22.

1. Our position

We respect intellectual property rights and expect submitters to do the same. Releases frequently include images, footage and third-party data, and responsibility for having the rights to that material sits with the submitter under our terms of service.

2. Filing a notice

Send a written notice to our designated agent: PressCosmos Media Network, DMCA Agent, 2261 Market Street, Suite 4800, San Francisco, CA 94114, US, or email support@presscosmos.com with the subject line "DMCA Notice". The notice must include:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed
  • The exact URL of the allegedly infringing material
  • Your name, address, telephone number and email address
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner
  • Your physical or electronic signature

3. What we do next

We remove or disable access to the identified material promptly, notify the submitter, and forward a copy of the notice to them. Removal does not erase the inbox record: notices are retained as evidence.

4. Counter-notice

If your release was removed and you believe the removal was wrong — for example because you hold a valid licence or the use is fair — you may send a counter-notice with the same level of detail and consent to the jurisdiction of a competent court. We may restore the material after ten business days unless the original complainant notifies us of a filed action.

5. Repeat infringers

Accounts that receive repeated substantiated notices are suspended, and persistent offenders are terminated. We do not accept a submission from a suspended account under a different name.

6. Syndicated copies

Releases are distributed to independent outlets. If your content is removed here but appears elsewhere, that outlet is a separate organisation and the request must be made to it. We will provide you with the delivery list for the release in question so you know where to look.

Questions about this policy? Write to support@presscosmos.com or post to PressCosmos Media Network, 2261 Market Street, Suite 4800, San Francisco, CA 94114, US.
