The three disciplines

AEO, GEO and LLMO — what each one actually means

AEO

Answer Engine Optimisation

Optimising for the machine that answers a question directly.

  • A 40–60 word answer block near the top of the release
  • Question-shaped subheadings and FAQ pairs
  • FAQPage structured data on every FAQ section
  • Tables and lists that survive being extracted

GEO

Generative Engine Optimisation

Optimising to be cited inside a generated response, with attribution.

  • Independent corroboration across syndicated outlets
  • Entity-consistent naming of your organisation
  • Original data points that are worth quoting
  • Stable canonical URLs so citations do not rot

LLMO

Large Language Model Optimisation

Optimising what a model knows and says about your brand.

  • Organization schema with identity, location and contacts
  • llms.txt guidance plus an open crawler policy
  • Machine-readable feeds and a clean sitemap
  • Clear, factual, unembellished claims

Caveats

What we cannot do

We can make your announcement retrievable, answerable, attributable and corroborated. We cannot force a language model to prefer you over a competitor, and neither can anyone else who is being straight with you.

What we will do is measure honestly: if your citation rate does not move, the report will say so, and we will tell you what we would change next time.

No prompt injection, no manipulation We do not bury instructions in copy, stuff hidden text, or attempt to game retrieval. It breaks eventually, and it is a reputational risk we will not take on your behalf.
Results are lagged Model freshness varies. Expect 2–8 weeks before a change in retrievability reliably shows up in citation share.
