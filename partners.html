Company

The network behind the distribution.

PressCosmos distributes through contracted editorial and feed partners. We publish the categories we contract with rather than a logo wall we cannot substantiate.

Become a partnerDistribution details
Quick answer PressCosmos contracts distribution through newswire partners, regional and metro press desks, industry trade publications, broadcaster and podcast networks, news aggregators, financial data feeds and AI retrieval systems. Partner names are disclosed to customers in the delivery log for each release.

Network

Partner categories

Newswire and feed partners

Contracted lanes carrying releases to national and international newswire feeds and data terminals.

Regional and metro press

Local dailies, business journals and city desks reached through regional routing.

Industry trade publications

Sector-specific titles across twelve verticals, each with its own editorial gate.

Broadcast and podcast networks

Audio and video desks that pick up newsworthy announcements for segments.

Aggregators and news apps

Distribution platforms and news applications that surface releases to consumer audiences.

AI and retrieval systems

Machine-readable feeds and open crawler access enabling citation inside AI answers.

Why we do not list partner logos Using a media company's trademark to imply a commercial relationship that is not documented is a legal risk and a credibility risk. Every customer receives the actual delivery list for their own release, which is more useful than a logo wall anyway.
