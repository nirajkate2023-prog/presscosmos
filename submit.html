Before you submit

The four things that get a release approved faster

A number that matters

One specific, verifiable figure in the first paragraph. It is what reporters quote.

A sourced claim

Superlatives need a citation. "Highest-rated" requires a source or it gets cut.

A usable contact

A monitored inbox, not a form. Journalists work to deadlines.

Rights-cleared assets

Media must be yours to distribute, or it is rejected on sight.

PressCosmos is a press release distribution and AI visibility platform. You write or upload an announcement, our editors review it, and we syndicate it to newswire partners, trade media, regional press and AI answer engines while tracking every pickup, backlink and AI citation you earn.

Traditional wires optimise for a press release existing on the internet. PressCosmos optimises for your announcement being found, quoted and repeated — by journalists, by search engines and by AI assistants. Every release ships with answer-engine formatting, structured data and entity markup by default.

Median time from approved submission to first live pickup is 42 minutes on Amplify and Newsmaker plans. Launch plan releases go live within four business hours, and the crisis lane publishes in under 60 minutes at any hour.
